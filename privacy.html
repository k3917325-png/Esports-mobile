Privacy Policy – GameNest Rewards

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · App package: com.sportsreward.app

This Privacy Policy explains how GameNest Rewards ("the app", "we") collects, uses and protects your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google we receive your email address, display name and a unique account ID.
  • Approximate location: your country, derived from your IP address (not GPS), used to show the correct currency, reward rates and to apply regional rules.
  • Device and security data: a hashed device identifier and IP/VPN checks, used only to prevent fraud, duplicate accounts and abuse.
  • Gameplay data: scores, coins, reward points (RP), game history, daily bonus and withdrawal requests.
  • Payout details: if you request a withdrawal, the payout detail you enter (for example UPI ID, phone number, email or wallet ID), used only to process that payout.
  • Advertising data: our ad partners may collect the Android advertising ID, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.

2. How we use your information

To create and verify your account, calculate rewards and leaderboards, process withdrawals and gift cards, prevent fraud and cheating, provide support, show ads, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) stores account and gameplay data. Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity LevelPlay and its mediation partner ad networks show rewarded and banner ads and may collect advertising ID and device data. Unity Privacy Policy

4. Ads

The app is free and funded by ads. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to claim a reward. Ad partners may use the advertising ID for ad delivery and measurement; you can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Rewards are promotional

The app has no deposits, entry fees or in-app purchases. It is not gambling or real-money gaming. Reward points have no cash value until redeemed under the app's rules.

6. Data retention and security

We keep your data while your account is active. We use reasonable security measures, but no internet service is 100% secure. Payment/withdrawal records may be kept as long as required for tax, legal and fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Delete your account and data

You can delete your account and associated data at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. You can also request deletion by email at sportsrewardhelp@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Deleted data is removed except records we must legally retain.

8. Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
